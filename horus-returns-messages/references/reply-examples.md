# Reply examples

Short, warm and factual. Sign off as the store (the eBay username) unless the user has a name they use.
Fill the brackets from the sale. Never send without the user's approval.

**Where is my order (shipped)**

> Hi [buyer], thanks for your order! It shipped on [shipped_at date] with [carrier]. Tracking number
> [tracking.number]. It can take a day for the tracking to update. Let me know if anything looks off.

**Where is my order (purchased, no tracking yet)**

> Hi [buyer], thanks for your patience! Your order has been dispatched, and the tracking number will
> appear on your eBay order as soon as the carrier scans it, usually within 1–2 business days.

**Wants to cancel (still PENDING, user approved the cancel)**

> Hi [buyer], no problem. I've cancelled the order, and eBay will refund you automatically.

**Wants to cancel (already purchased or shipped)**

> Hi [buyer], sorry, your order has already shipped, so I can't cancel it now. Once it arrives you
> can open a return through eBay and I'll take care of it.

**Item damaged / not as described**

> Hi [buyer], I'm sorry it arrived like that. Please open a return from your eBay purchase history
> ("Return this item") and I'll get it sorted out right away.

**Return label question**

> Hi [buyer], the return label is attached to your eBay return case. You'll find it under the
> return in your purchase history. Drop it off within [N] days and the refund is processed once
> it's received.

Don't write:

- "You'll get your refund today" (Horus may not have finished the source return).
- "This ships from Amazon/Walmart…" or anything about where the item is bought.
- "Email me at…", "Pay via…": nothing that moves the conversation off eBay.
